Back in my school days, I was really passionate about gaming. One day, my dad asked me if I knew how the bots in those games worked. I had no idea what he meant, so he introduced me to the world of Artificial Intelligence.

## Specific Areas of AI I'm Passionate About

### Computer Vision (Primary Passion)
I'm particularly drawn to **Computer Vision** because working with images and videos—analyzing, identifying, and manipulating them—feels both fascinating and fun to me.

**Why Computer Vision?**
- Visual data is intuitive and engaging
- Wide range of applications (from gaming to healthcare)
- Combines creativity with technical depth
- Rapidly evolving field with new techniques emerging constantly
- Can see immediate, tangible results

**Areas Within Computer Vision:**
- Image classification and object detection
- Optical Character Recognition (OCR)
- Multi-modal learning (combining vision with text)
- Video analysis and understanding
- Generative models for images

### Other Areas of Interest
- **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** Fascinated by LLMs like ChatGPT
- **Multi-Modal AI:** Combining different data types (vision + text, audio + vision)
- **Predictive Modeling:** Using AI for forecasting and early intervention
- **MLOps & Production AI:** Building scalable, production-ready systems
